# 🌙 Until Our Universes Meet Again 💜

*A little corner of the universe dedicated to JoongDunk and AouBoom.*

## ✨ About the Project

**Until Our Universes Meet Again** is a personal fan-made website created as a heartfelt tribute to Joong Archen & Dunk Natachai and Aou Thanaboon & Boom Tharatorn.

Some stories may take unexpected turns, but the memories, happiness, and moments they gave us will always have a special place in our hearts.

This website is my little way of saying thank you for the beautiful memories and wishing them happiness in their individual journeys. It's not necessarily the end of every story—just a little pause, with hope for whatever the future holds. 🌌

## 💜 Features

- 🌌 A beautiful cosmic-inspired design
- ⭐ Animated stars and a dreamy night-sky atmosphere
- 🫶 Dedicated sections for JoongDunk and AouBoom
- 💌 Heartfelt farewell messages
- 🌙 An interactive wish feature
- 📱 Responsive design for different screen sizes

## 🛠️ Built With

- HTML5
- CSS3
- JavaScript
